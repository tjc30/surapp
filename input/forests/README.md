# Tree-cover change, 2000–2020

Source: Potapov et al. (2022), *The Global 2000–2020 Land Cover and Land Use
Change Dataset Derived From the Landsat Archive: First Results*, Table 1.
https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/remote-sensing/articles/10.3389/frsen.2022.856903/full
DOI: 10.3389/frsen.2022.856903. Published 13 April 2022. CC BY.

Manually transcribed on 15 October 2026. Table values in million square
kilometres were multiplied by 100 to obtain million hectares (Mha).
Seven rows: World and the six regions as named by the source (Australia is not
relabeled Oceania). World is a source total, not a sum of displayed regions.
The source rounds loss, gain and net change separately. Keep reported net
change; do not recalculate it from rounded gross figures. In particular,
131 - 231 = -100 Mha, while the published net change rounds to -101 Mha.

These are endpoint changes for 2000–2020, not annual observations. Trees at
least five metres tall are included, including natural and planted tree cover.
Gain is not a restoration-only measure. It includes natural regeneration and
plantations; loss is not synonymous with permanent deforestation. Do not
combine these estimates with a different annual tree-cover-loss product.
